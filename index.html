Sisi is Kiswahili for us. It is a public participation site for Kenya's 47 counties, piloting in Nairobi City County with Kilimani Ward since September 2026.

What Sisi does

Register once with your county and ward. After that you receive every county notice, draft bill and budget hearing that affects your ward, and you can respond online instead of finding a weekday free to travel into town for a physical baraza.

A CIDP or a CBEF session is not a closed meeting between officials. Under the Constitution it is built on the voice of every mwananchi — that is the point of the name.

Who can take part

Any Kenyan citizen who lives, works or owns property in Kilimani Ward can register and comment on Sisi. You do not need to attend in person — a written submission carries the same weight in the official record as one made from the floor of a hall.

Sisi is currently piloting in Nairobi City County, starting with Kilimani Ward in Dagoretti North. More wards join as their county assemblies confirm public participation calendars.

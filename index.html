What Sisi does

Every notice, delivered

Once you register, county notices, draft bills and budget hearings for your ward reach you by SMS or email — you no longer have to watch for a paper notice board.

Respond from anywhere

Submit a written comment or vote on a ward project instead of finding a weekday free to travel into town for a physical baraza.

Counted the same

A written submission carries the same weight in the official record as one made from the floor of a hall — that is the constitutional point of the name.

CIDP and CBEF sessions are not closed meetings between officials. Under the Constitution they are built on the voice of every mwananchi.

I used to miss every ward meeting because of my shift at work. Reading the notice online and sending in my comment took ten minutes.

Who can take part

Any Kenyan citizen who lives, works or owns property in Kilimani Ward can register and comment on Sisi. You do not need to attend in person — a written submission carries the same weight in the official record as one made from the floor of a hall.

Sisi is currently piloting in Nairobi City County, starting with Kilimani Ward in Dagoretti North. More wards join as their county assemblies confirm public participation calendars.

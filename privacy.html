Data Protection Act, 2019

Privacy notice

Sisi collects only what it needs to send you Kilimani Ward notices and to record your comments in the official participation record — nothing more.

This follows the data minimisation principle in Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019 — a form whose purpose is "send you ward notices" should ask for a ward and a contact channel, not a full identity file.

What we collect

  • Your county and ward, so notices reach the right resident list.
  • Your full name, so your submission can be attributed in the public record if you choose to comment.
  • One contact channel — email, phone, or both — matching the notification preference you select.
  • The topics you ask to hear about, so we do not send you everything.
  • If you choose to write one, your view on the current budget or development plan.

What we do not collect

  • National ID number.
  • Date of birth.
  • Physical address beyond your ward.
  • Payment or banking information.

Identity verification, if it is ever needed for a formal objection process, belongs at a separate, encrypted step — not folded into a notification sign-up.

Who can see it

Your details are visible to the county officials handling the CBEF or CADP process your submission relates to. Sisi does not sell, share for advertising, or transfer your data to any other organisation.

Your rights

Under the Data Protection Act, 2019 you can ask to see the data Sisi holds about you, ask for it to be corrected, or ask for it to be deleted once the participation window it relates to has closed. Write to habari@sisi.org for any of these.

If you believe your data has been mishandled, you can also raise it directly with Kenya's Office of the Data Protection Commissioner.

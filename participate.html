Terms used on this page

CIDP
County Integrated Development Plan — a county's five-year development plan, reviewed at its mid-term.
CADP
County Annual Development Plan — the CIDP broken into one financial year, drafted each September and used to prepare the following year's budget.
CBEF
County Budget and Economic Forum — the forum where county government and resident representatives agree budget priorities before they go to the county assembly.
Ward development allocation
The share of the county development budget set aside for projects proposed and ranked within a single ward.
